# Privacy Policy — JFT-Basic Prep

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy explains what information the JFT-Basic Prep app ("the App") handles. The App is operated by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we").

## Information stored on your device
The App saves your study progress (scores, answered questions, settings such as language and furigana) **only on your device**. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone input.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are processed by **Google Play**. We never see your payment card details.
To manage subscriptions we use **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the App, purchase information (product, price, purchase and renewal dates) and basic device information (such as OS version and country/store). This is used only to check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases.
RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Text-to-speech
Listening questions are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine on your device (for example Google Speech Services). The text is processed according to that engine's own policy.

## Advertising
The App does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

## Children
The App is intended for adults preparing to work in Japan and is not directed to children under 13.

## Data deletion
Because progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings. To request deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at the address below.
